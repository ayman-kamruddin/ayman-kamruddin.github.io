Ultra-Processed People - Book Review

Ultra-processed foods (UPFs) are bad. The industry does not want you to believe this, research on the subject is biased, there are undeclared conflicts of interests between scientists and food conglomerates. UPFs are really bad not just for your health but also for the planet. This books presents an easily readable scientific study on the maleficence of UPFs.

UPFs are profit-driven products, and as a consequence what sells sells - no regards to health or the environment. To the extent that the false science is being published about the non-effects of UPFs. Worse, policy is being adopted - or the right policies are not being adopted - concerning this stuff we put into our bodies (often on a regular basis) - that is not well informed. People may think it’s about their willpower, while heavy marketing is much to blame. Read on to know more about this industrially produced addition that puts all our health at risk. Thank you, Dr. Tulleken, for taking the steps to counter all the madness big food corps are imposing on us.
